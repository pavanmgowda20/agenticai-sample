About the Event

The Agentic AI Summit 2026 is a landmark event bringing together innovators from academia and industry to explore the power of intelligent, autonomous systems.

Held in Bangalore, India, on 30 and 31 October, 2026, this two-day summit features expert keynotes, hands-on workshops, and interactive panels on agentic AI, multi-agent systems, and real-world applications.

Participants will gain practical insights from leading professionals, network globally, and develop skills to build and deploy next-generation AI agents. The Agentic AI Summit 2026 is the place where ideas meet innovation, shaping the future of autonomous intelligence.
